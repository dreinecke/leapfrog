# Third-party source

`src/t1bridge/` is the standalone public renderer protocol client copied from
`standardagents/touchbar-doom`. It was originally copied there from
`touchbar-rs` revision `ad63fba671c7ce2c2c3b6a9f3c04fad3d9aa8901`, Copyright
2026 Andrew Boyd, and is used under the MIT license retained at
`LICENSES/touchbar-client-MIT.txt`.

The protocol client implements T1Bridge's public renderer contract:
https://github.com/standardagents/t1bridge/blob/main/docs/interfaces.md
